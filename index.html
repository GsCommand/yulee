Local conditions

Why Yulee surfaces need the right preparation

Sun and rain. Florida heat, UV exposure and storm cycles can fade pavers and wash joint material from driveways and pool decks.

Humidity and shade. Shaded walls, pool enclosures and roof areas can hold moisture longer and develop algae or mildew faster.

Irrigation and staining. Overspray can leave mineral or rust-colored staining that should be identified before sealing.

Different materials. Concrete pavers, travertine, stucco and roofing materials should not all be cleaned with the same pressure or chemistry.

Process

A simple service process

  1. Review: confirm the surface, condition and problem areas.
  2. Prepare: protect surrounding areas and address stains or organic buildup.
  3. Clean: use a method appropriate for the surface.
  4. Repair or sand: complete agreed paver preparation where needed.
  5. Dry and verify: confirm conditions before sealing.
  6. Finish: apply the selected sealer or complete the soft-wash service.
  7. Walk through: review cure time, care and any follow-up notes.

Yulee paver sealing and exterior cleaning questions

What is included in a Yulee paver sealing project?

The scope depends on the surface, but paver projects typically begin with inspection and cleaning, followed by agreed joint preparation, drying and sealing when conditions are appropriate.

Do you seal pool decks and travertine?

Yes. Pool deck pavers and travertine are handled as separate surface types because moisture, traction, material sensitivity and sealer selection matter.

Do you also offer house and roof washing?

Yes. House washing and roof washing are offered as soft-wash exterior cleaning services for appropriate surfaces.

How long does paver sealer need to cure?

Cure time varies with the product, weather, shade, moisture and project conditions. You will receive project-specific instructions before normal use resumes.
