Patio Paver Sealing Yulee and Pool-Area Pavers

Pool decks and patios often dry more slowly than open driveways, especially inside screened enclosures or shaded areas. Organic buildup, joint-sand loss near drains and cage edges, and previous sealer all affect preparation.

We treat pool deck paver sealing and patio paver sealing as surface-specific restoration work rather than simply applying another coat.
