Wildlight priorities

What we look for before sealing

Existing sealer

Fading, whitening, patchiness or failure can change the preparation required.

Joint loss

Heavy rain, runoff and prior cleaning can lower sand between pavers.

Staining

Irrigation, rust, organic buildup and tire marks are evaluated before sealing.

Movement

Sunken or loose pavers may need repair before the surface is sealed.

Moisture

Screened patios and shaded areas may require longer dry-down.

Finish

The sealer system is selected for the material, condition and desired appearance.
