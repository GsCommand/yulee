Professional driveway paver sealing starts with the surface condition

Driveway paver sealing in Yulee is more than pressure washing followed by another coat. Florida sun, heavy rain, irrigation staining, tire traffic, joint-sand loss and older coatings can all change how a driveway should be cleaned, re-sanded and sealed.

HydroSeal evaluates the existing surface first so paver cleaning and sealing Yulee FL is matched to the actual coating, drainage, stains, repairs and joint condition.

Paver Cleaning Yulee FL

Organic buildup, tire marks, irrigation residue and loose contamination should be addressed before joint work or sealer. Cleaning pressure and chemistry are selected for the paver surface and the condition of any previous coating.

Paver Re-Sanding Yulee FL

Heavy rain, runoff and previous cleaning can lower joint levels. Re-sanding restores depleted joints where appropriate before a joint-stabilizing sealer is applied.

Paver Restoration Yulee FL

Whitening, flaking, severe fading, sunken pavers or failed old sealer can turn a simple maintenance reseal into a restoration project that needs additional preparation.
