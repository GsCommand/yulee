Driveway sealing built around surface condition

Yulee driveway pavers can fade, lose joint sand, collect tire marks and develop staining from irrigation or organic buildup. A good result starts with identifying those conditions before sealer is applied.

The process is based on the actual paver surface, existing coating, drainage, stains and joint condition rather than treating every driveway the same.

Service

What a driveway paver sealing project can include

Cleaning and preparation

Surface cleaning and targeted preparation for buildup, staining and problem areas.

Joint-sand work

Restore depleted joints where appropriate before sealing.

Sealer application

Apply sealer only after the surface is properly prepared and dry enough for the selected system.

Driveway conditions

What we look for before sealing

Existing sealer. Whitening, patchy sheen or failed coatings can change the preparation required.

Joint loss. Heavy rain, pressure cleaning and traffic can lower sand between pavers.

Stains. Irrigation, rust, oil and organic staining should be identified before sealing.

Movement. Sunken or rocking pavers may need repair before the sealing step.

Process

How driveway paver sealing works

  1. Inspect: pavers, joints, stains, drainage and existing coating.
  2. Pre-treat: address agreed stain or organic-growth areas.
  3. Clean: prepare the driveway surface.
  4. Repair: complete agreed paver repairs where needed.
  5. Re-sand: restore joint levels where appropriate.
  6. Dry: confirm conditions are ready for sealer.
  7. Seal: apply the selected system at the proper rate.

Questions

Yulee driveway paver sealing questions

How do I know if my driveway is ready to be resealed?

Look at the condition of the previous sealer, joint sand, staining and overall surface. A driveway should be evaluated before simply adding another coat.

Can you address missing joint sand?

Yes, joint-sand loss can be addressed as part of the preparation when the paver system and project conditions are suitable.

What if the old sealer is white or cloudy?

Cloudy or whitening sealer can indicate coating or moisture problems. Those areas should be evaluated before resealing.

Can you seal over irrigation or rust staining?

Staining should be identified and treated where appropriate before sealing. Sealing over visible staining can make later correction more difficult.

When can I drive on the driveway again?

Cure time depends on the product, weather and site conditions. Project-specific vehicle guidance should be followed after sealing.
