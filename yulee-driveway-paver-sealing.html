Do you provide paver re-sanding in Yulee FL?

What paver joint sand do you use?

The joint material is selected for the paver system and project conditions, and installed after proper cleaning and preparation.

Can you seal over white or cloudy old sealer?

Cloudy or whitening sealer should be evaluated first because adding another coat may make a failed coating problem worse.

Is driveway paver sealing different from paver restoration?

Yes. A maintenance seal is simpler. Restoration can include heavier cleaning, stain treatment, repairs, re-sanding or failed-sealer preparation.
