What we inspect

Before a Del Webb paver project begins

Coating

Check for fading, whitening, patchiness or failed old sealer.

Joints

Measure low or washed-out sand and determine whether re-sanding is appropriate.

Stains

Identify irrigation, rust, tire, organic or other discoloration before sealing.

Repairs

Note loose, sunken or shifted pavers that should be corrected first.

Moisture

Confirm the surface and joints are dry enough for the selected sealer.

Finish

Select a compatible system based on the pavers, prior coating and desired appearance.
