Surface-specific cleaning

Pressure where it belongs, soft washing where it does not

Concrete and other durable hard surfaces can often tolerate pressure washing. Stucco, painted finishes, siding and roofs may require a lower-pressure approach. The surface determines the method.

Avoid damage

Why one cleaning method does not fit every surface

Aggressive pressure can damage softer finishes, disturb paver joints or create visible marks. Proper exterior cleaning uses the lowest effective pressure paired with chemistry appropriate to the material and buildup.
