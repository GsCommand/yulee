Travertine Needs a Stone-Specific Approach

Travertine should not be treated like ordinary concrete or brick pavers. Surface finish, open fill, moisture, staining and previous products can all affect how the stone should be cleaned and sealed.

The goal is to improve protection while respecting the natural stone and avoiding aggressive shortcuts that can create unnecessary damage or discoloration.

Service

What Travertine Sealing Can Include

Stone-Safe Cleaning

Cleaning methods selected for natural stone instead of defaulting to aggressive pressure.

Surface Evaluation

Check moisture, open fill, staining, previous products and damaged areas before sealing.

Breathable Protection

Apply a surface-appropriate sealer only after the stone is prepared and conditions are suitable.

Stone conditions

What We Evaluate Before Sealing Travertine

Moisture. Pool decks and shaded lanais can retain moisture longer than open hardscape.

Open fill. Voids or worn filler can affect appearance and should be noted before work begins.

Staining. Organic, irrigation or other discoloration may need targeted treatment.

Existing finish. Previous sealers or coatings can affect compatibility and preparation.

Process

How Yulee Travertine Sealing Works

  1. Inspect: review stone condition, moisture, stains and previous products.
  2. Protect: prepare surrounding pool, landscape and adjacent areas.
  3. Clean: use a method appropriate for natural stone.
  4. Evaluate: review open fill or damaged areas after cleaning.
  5. Dry: allow suitable drying time.
  6. Seal: apply the selected breathable protection.
  7. Review: provide cure-time and care guidance.

Questions

Yulee Travertine Sealing Questions

Is travertine sealed the same way as concrete pavers?

No. Travertine is natural stone and should be evaluated for finish, moisture, fill and existing products before cleaning or sealing.

Can travertine around a pool be sealed?

Yes. Pool-deck travertine can be sealed with a surface-appropriate product when preparation and moisture conditions are suitable.

What if my travertine has holes or missing fill?

Open fill should be identified during inspection. Repair needs can be discussed separately from the cleaning and sealing scope.

Can staining be treated before sealing?

Some staining can be treated, but results depend on stain type, penetration and stone condition.
